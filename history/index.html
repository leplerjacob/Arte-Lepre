About

Three generations, one craft

From a workshop in Naples in 1959 to Washington, D.C. today.

Since 1959

With over 60 years of experience and three generations later, the Lepre family run artisan restoration workshop has been a staple in Naples, Italy since 1959, specializing in the restoration of Italian, French and English style antique furniture and cabinet making.

“…the legacy of his family, through the love of his craft.”

The Lepre Family

A family timeline

  1. 1959

    A workshop in Naples

    The Lepre family opens its artisan restoration workshop in Naples, specializing in the restoration of Italian, French and English style antique furniture and cabinet making.

  2. Learning the craft

    Raffaele Lepre

    Raffaele learned the craft from a young age, thanks to his uncle Sassano Giacomo, an artisan cabinet maker.

  3. The next generation

    Alfredo & Fabio

    Mr. Lepre passed his enthusiasm and passion for all things antique to his two sons, Alfredo and Fabio Lepre.

  4. The 2010s

    Washington, D.C.

    Fabio Lepre emigrated to the Greater Washington D.C. area for a special someone that won his heart, bringing over 20 years in antique restoration, conservation and trading. He worked in a prestigious antiques gallery in Georgetown.

  5. Today

    Arte Lepre

    Fabio slowly expanded his business to focus on the legacy of his family through the love of his craft. Today Arte Lepre has workshops in Kensington, Maryland and in Naples.

Our Method

Arte Lepre guarantees the quality of antiques through meticulous restoration by hand, using exclusively traditional methods such as spirit polishing and shellac, beeswax polishing, and the integration of missing parts with antique wood, returning each piece to its original state.
