Services

Restoration & conservation

Every piece is restored by hand in the Kensington workshop, using exclusively traditional methods and natural materials.

  1. I

    Refinishing & French Polishing

    Surfaces cleaned and brought back to life by hand, keeping the original patina wherever possible.

    • Removal of old paint and dirt only where necessary, avoiding abrasive or acidic products
    • French polishing by hand with shellac dissolved in spirit
    • Beeswax finishes for a uniform, protective patina
  2. II

    Veneer, Marquetry & Inlay Repair

    Lifting, cracked or missing veneer re-laid, and losses in marquetry and inlay replaced to match.

    • Veneer re-laid with natural hot glue
    • Missing marquetry and inlay rebuilt in period-matched woods
    • Fillers made from colored earth pigments matched to the original tone
  3. III

    Structural & Woodworm Repair

    Loose joints, broken legs and missing parts rebuilt so the piece is sound and usable again.

    • Consolidation of worm-eaten, detached or degraded parts
    • Missing parts reconstructed in antique wood
    • Woodworm treated by injection with an odorless, colorless insecticide
  4. IV

    House Calls, Pickup & Delivery

    Fabio can assess pieces in your home and arrange for them to be collected and returned.

    • In-home assessment of furniture that is hard to move
    • Collection from and delivery back to your home
    • Advice on care once your piece is back in place

Our Approach

Two kinds of intervention

I

Conservative Restoration

Consolidation of the existing parts of the furniture — worm-eaten, detached or degraded — without necessarily returning it to its original condition.

II

Integrative Restoration

Reconstruction of furniture that is missing fundamental parts, returning it to its original structure.

Our Method

The stages of restoration

  1. I

    Stripping

    Removal, if necessary, of old paint and dirt, preserving the original patina — the colour the piece has acquired over time — as much as possible. Only in integrative interventions, and never with abrasive or acidic substances.

  2. II

    Woodworm treatment

    Injection of anti-woodworm products into the holes in the wood, using permethrin, an odorless and colorless insecticide.

  3. III

    Restoration of the veneer

    Integration, where necessary, of ancient material using natural hot glue. The veneer is the finishing layer: a thin sheet of precious wood no more than one millimeter thick.

  4. IV

    Reconstruction of missing parts

    In integrative restorations, the furniture is returned to its original structure.

  5. V

    Filler

    A mixture of colored earth pigments matched as closely as possible to the original tone of the wood.

  6. VI

    Shellac finish

    Shellac, a natural resin dissolved in alcohol and mixed with other resins, is applied to smooth and polish the wood, restoring the patina of the past.

  7. VII

    Beeswax coating

    A layer of beeswax gives a uniform patina that protects the surface of the wood.
