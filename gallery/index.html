Before & after

A selection of restorations from our workshops. Each plate shows a piece as it arrived, above, and after restoration, below.

  1. Before & after

    Plate I

    Commode Louis XVI Sorrento

    Period late 18th century. With marquetry in walnut, rosewood, bois de rose, maple and olive, the rectangular top above a conforming case enclosing three full drawers, raised on straight tapering legs, the whole elaborately decorated with scrolling leafage.

  2. Before & after

    Plate II

    Walnut Writing Desk

    Period 18th century, Tuscany-Lucca.

  3. Before & after

    Plate III

    Neapolitan Louis XV Writing Desk

    Period mid-18th century, walnut-plated, with bois de rose marquetry, and purple ebony sliding top.

  4. Before & after

    Plate IV

    Neapolitan Louis Philippe Coffee Table

    Period early 19th century, octagonal top with boxwood marquetry.

    Plate V

    Napoleone III Chest with 3 Drawers

    Period mid-19th century chest in bois de rose wood, decorated with rich marquetry of different fruitwoods with musical motifs.

  6. Before & after

    Plate VI

    Charles X Smith Console

    Period mid-19th century. Feather mahogany with fine maple wood inlay.
